## 二、9 个差距点（逐条对照，带证据）

### 差距 1｜例子是"假设"，不是"事实"（最致命）

| | 内容 |
|:--|:--|
| **Joe** | "For example, in a team project, different members can find different solutions that one person might miss." |
| **范文** | "A history mural in a local park, for example, can remind residents of their shared past…"／"A single factory can release more waste in a day than thousands of residents produce in a year." |
| **问题** | Joe 的例子换成任何题目都成立 → **信息量为零**；范文的例子有**地点、有对象、有量级**，读者能"看见" |
| **改法** | 例子里必须出现至少一个**具体名词 + 一个数字或场景**。写不出真实例子时，用"具体化假设"：*"in a five-person marketing team splitting one campaign…"* |

### 差距 2｜因果链只有 2 步，范文是 3–4 步

| | 内容 |
|:--|:--|
| **Joe** | "working together brings more ideas → it is easier to solve problems"（**2 步，且是常识**） |
| **范文** | "employees are involved in decisions → bring **diverse perspectives** → improves **problem-solving** → strengthens **team cohesion**：people support a plan they helped shape → **psychological safety** → raise concerns before they become **costly mistakes**"（**5 步**） |
| **改法** | 每个理由后强制补一句 **"which means…" / "as a result…"**，把链条延长一到两环 |

### 差距 3｜抽象名词缺失（口语动词 vs 学术名词）

| Joe 用 | 范文用 |
|:----|:----|
| more ideas | **diverse perspectives** |
| work harder / do their best | **stronger commitment** |
| solve problems | **improve problem-solving** |
| afraid to speak up | **psychological safety / stop offering feedback** |
| nobody gives feedback → bad decisions | **making poor decisions in silence / costly mistakes** |

> **规律**：满分范文把"动作"升级成"**概念**"——这是 5.0 与 5.5 的分水岭。

### 差距 4｜让步段只"否定"，不"限定"（辩证深度）

| | 内容 |
|:--|:--|
| **Joe** | "Andrew argues that an authoritative style is good for high-pressure situations. **However, its main problem is that if the leader is too strict, employees might be afraid to speak up.**"（直接换靶子：从"高压场景"跑到"太严格"） |
| **范文** | "**Andrew is right that** an authoritative approach can be useful in high-pressure situations… **However, this style should be temporary rather than the default.**"（**承认有效性 → 限定适用范围**） |
| **改法** | 让步三拍：**① 承认对方在 X 场景有效 ② 限定它不该是默认 ③ 给出边界条件**。不要为了反驳而转移话题 |

### 差距 5｜句式单一（几乎全是"主语+动词+宾语"）

Joe 全篇连接词：`First / Second / However / Then / Therefore`（5 个，全是**顺序词**）。
范文的句式工具：

| 手法 | 范文示例 |
|:----|:----|
| 分词开头 | "**Instead of spending** the week worrying about deadlines, we could explore local markets…" |
| 冒号解释 | "…strengthens team cohesion**:** people are more likely to support a plan they helped shape." |
| 对比连接 | "**Unlike** many social programs, public art is visible… **By contrast**, consumer pressure is limited…" |
| 让步从句 | "**Although** Paul emphasizes individual action, the scale of industrial pollution makes…" |
| 名词化 | "the **scale** of industrial pollution / **enforceable** standards" |

> **硬指标**：一篇 130 词的文章，**至少用 3 种不同句式**，顺序连接词不超过 2 个。

### 差距 6｜结尾是"复述"，不是"升华"

| | 内容 |
|:--|:--|
| **Joe** | "Therefore, collaborative leadership is better for a company."（= 开头原句换词） |
| **范文** | "Overall, collaborative leadership is more **sustainable** because it **combines better ideas with stronger commitment**, while still **allowing leaders to act decisively when necessary**."（**给出更高一层判断 + 保留让步**） |
| **改法** | 结尾写"**为什么它长期更优**"，并留一个"但仍然……"的尾巴 |

### 差距 7｜Email 缺 Subject 行（格式直接扣分风险）

范文有：`To: Alex` / `Subject: A Spring Break Trip We'll Both Love`
Joe 直接从 `Hi Alex,` 开始。**邮件题先写 Subject，再写称呼。**

### 差距 8｜Email 的"便利"没有落地

| | 内容 |
|:--|:--|
| **Joe** | "The package includes round-trip train tickets, two nights in a hotel, and a guided city tour. **It makes everything super convenient.**" |
| **范文** | "…**so we wouldn't have to plan every detail ourselves.** It seems convenient and **affordable**, and it would let us **make the most of our short holiday**." |
| **问题** | "super convenient" 是**评价词**，不是**证据**；范文把便利翻译成"不用自己规划每个细节" |
| **改法** | 形容词后面必须跟一句"**具体意味着什么**" |

### 差距 9｜Email 没有画面感与情绪递进

Joe：`fun vacation` / `amazing street food` / `relax`
范文：`explore local markets, try new dishes, and simply relax together`（三个动词短语=画面）+ 结尾 `I'd be really excited to go with you!`（情绪落点）

## 三、满分范文的 7 条可复制特征（要领）

1. **一句表态，两个限定词**：always 少用，用 *generally / in most business settings*，显得审慎。
2. **每个理由 = 主张 + 机制 + 例证**（机制句永远是最容易漏的）。
3. **例子要"能画出来"**：有人、有地、有数、有后果。
4. **让步三步**：承认有效 → 限定范围 → 指出代价。
5. **抽象名词替换口语动词**：ideas→perspectives；harder→commitment；afraid→psychological safety。
6. **句式三件套**：分词开头 / 冒号解释 / 对比连接（Unlike, By contrast）。
7. **结尾给"长期判断 + 保留尾巴"**，不复述开头。

## 四、改写示范（同一内容，升级版）

### A. 学术讨论（Joe 版 → 升级版，约 135 词）

**升级版：**
> I agree with Kelly that collaborative leadership is generally more effective. When employees help shape decisions, they contribute **diverse perspectives** that a single leader may overlook—**which means** problems get solved before they escalate, not after. **Beyond better ideas**, collaboration builds **commitment**: people defend a plan they helped create, so execution needs less supervision. Andrew is right that an authoritative style can be decisive **in a crisis**; **however**, it should be **the exception rather than the default**, because a leader who never hears bad news eventually makes decisions in the dark. **Overall**, collaboration is more **sustainable**: it produces better information and stronger ownership, while still allowing firm direction when speed matters.

**改动点**：① 加"which means"机制句 ② diverse perspectives / commitment / in the dark（抽象化）③ 让步改为"承认+限定" ④ 结尾给长期判断 ⑤ 冒号与破折号解释。

### B. Email（补 Subject + 具体化，约 95 词）

> **Subject: A Spring Break Trip We'd Both Enjoy**
> Hi Alex,
> I found a travel package to a nearby city for spring break, and I immediately thought of us. The city is known for its street food—something we both love—and it would be a real break from exam pressure. **Instead of spending the week worrying about deadlines**, we could wander through local markets, try new dishes, and simply relax together.
> The package covers round-trip train tickets, two hotel nights, and a guided city tour, **so we wouldn't have to plan anything ourselves**. Shall we book it this weekend, before the price goes up? I'd be really excited to go with you.
> Best regards,
> Joe

## 五、优先级（26 天内只抓三个）

| 优先级 | 训练点 | 每天可做的动作 |
|:---:|:----|:----|
| 🥇 | **机制句**（which means / as a result） | 每写完一个理由，强迫补一句"所以呢" |
| 🥈 | **具体例证** | 每个例子必须写出"几个人/什么地方/什么数字" |
| 🥉 | **让步三步** | 每天挑一个对立观点，练"承认—限定—代价"三句话 |

> 这三条改掉，写作从 5.0 稳到 5.5 的路径最清晰；其余（句式、词汇）跟着自然提升。
